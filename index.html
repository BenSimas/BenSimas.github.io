THIRD GAME TITLE

Combat Racing · Unity · C#

Featured Project

A combat-focused racing game built around vehicle physics, customisation, AI and persistent progression.

As a solo developer, I designed and implemented the game's core gameplay systems, from physics-based vehicle handling and combat to vehicle customisation, AI, progression and save systems.

Key Programming Work

  • Vehicle simulation: physics-based vehicle handling using Unity WheelColliders, with driving systems designed around racing and combat.
  • Vehicle customisation: modular system allowing individual vehicle parts, weapons and upgrades to be changed independently.
  • AI: driving and combat behaviour for AI-controlled vehicles.
  • Combat: weapon systems, shooting, damage handling and explosions.
  • Progression & persistence: save/load system tracking unlocked parts, upgrades and player progression.
  • Game architecture: bootstrap scene and initialization flow for persistent game managers and systems.
  • UI: deep menu and vehicle customisation interface supporting the game's progression and customization systems.
  • Cinematics & presentation: Cinemachine, Timeline, audio systems, post-processing and environmental presentation.

Additional work: track design, level construction, world building, scenery and gameplay presentation.

Titanofonias

Soulslike action RPG focused on responsive combat, enemy AI and character systems.

  • Custom kinematic character controller handling movement, ground detection, slopes, gravity and collision resolution.
  • Hierarchical player state machine managing movement, combat, blocking, ranged attacks, hit reactions and dodging.
  • Animation-synchronized combat systems controlling hit detection, stamina, attack transitions and timing.
  • Modular enemy AI supporting shared behaviours across multiple enemy archetypes and bosses.
  • Event-driven audio system supporting contextual SFX and dynamic music transitions.
  • URP and rendering optimizations to reduce GPU load while maintaining visual quality.

Conquest: Raid Warfare

Strategy battle simulator and my first shipped Unity project.

  • Custom grid-based unit placement with coordinate mapping, tile validation and visual feedback.
